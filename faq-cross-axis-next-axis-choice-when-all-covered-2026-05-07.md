# FAQ — how do we choose the next axis when all 10 already have exposure? (2026-05-07)

## Short answer

Do not choose the next axis by file count. Choose the axis where a newcomer is most likely to make the wrong first move despite existing hub/path/FAQ/source/process/coverage exposure.

## Decision order

1. **User-risk first** — prefer the axis with the highest risk of privacy leakage, premature disclosure, legal overclaim, or wrong addressee.
2. **First-action ambiguity second** — prefer the axis where the first 30 minutes are least obvious even after reading the existing route.
3. **Cross-axis confusion third** — prefer the axis that is often mistaken for another axis and needs a better tie-breaker.
4. **Only then density** — if risk and ambiguity tie, improve the thinnest coverage route.

## Practical tie-breakers

| If the newcomer asks... | Improve next |
|---|---|
| “Is this public, partially public, or internal?” | 정보공개기록관리 / 홍보대응 boundary route |
| “Who owns this answer?” | 민원대응 / 국회대응 / 법령대응 routing route |
| “What evidence must be attached before drafting?” | 감사대응 / 예산대응 / 계약조달 source bundle route |
| “Is this a staffing, budget, or contract issue?” | 조직정원 / 예산대응 / 계약조달 cross-axis route |
| “Can I just make a new hub?” | Use [[router-existing-hub-entry-index-2026-05-07]] before expanding |
